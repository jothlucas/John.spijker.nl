About

From choir boy to Paradiso

I started out as a choir boy and took up drumming lessons at ten. After my high school band I became lead singer of the rock band Apple, and today I front the cover band The Peachkings, playing parties and festivals across the country.

But writing my own songs is what I love most – it's a real and ongoing source of joy in my life. Ever since the Amsterdam singer-songwriter competition Mooie Noten in 2005, I've been playing my own songs everywhere, for everyone.

Influences: Prince, Stevie Wonder, The Beatles, Led Zeppelin, James Taylor, Van Halen.

  • Paradiso, AmsterdamSemi-finals of Mooie Noten, 2005
  • Melkweg, AmsterdamOpening act for ALO (Animal Liberation Orchestra), 2006
  • Support actFor Flaco Jimenez, Bløf, De Kast, Di-rect, Volumia and 16 Down
